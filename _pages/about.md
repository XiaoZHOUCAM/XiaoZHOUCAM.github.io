I am an Associate Professor at the Gaoling School of Artificial Intelligence, Renmin University of China. Previously, I was a Postdoctoral Fellow at the Senseable City Lab, Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT). I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Cambridge, where I was supervised by Professor Cecilia Mascolo (FREng).

My research focuses on trustworthy artificial intelligence for human and social decision-making. I am particularly interested in large language model reasoning, knowledge grounding, personalised and pluralistic alignment, causal modelling, and agentic systems. My recent work has also explored recommender systems, urban and social computing, and AI for health. My research has been recognised with the WWW 2026 Best Paper Award and a NeurIPS Spotlight. Broadly, I aim to develop AI systems that can reason effectively about information, people, and actions, while remaining reliable, context-sensitive, and responsive to diverse human needs and values.

Our Team
======

I lead the LUMOS AI Lab at the Gaoling School of Artificial Intelligence, Renmin University of China. Our group brings together postgraduate students working on large language models, trustworthy AI, recommender systems, urban and social computing, and related interdisciplinary problems.

Our research aims to develop intelligent systems that can reason effectively, adapt to diverse human needs and values, and support decision-making in complex human and social environments. We are particularly interested in work that combines methodological advances in AI with meaningful real-world applications. If you share these interests, please feel free to get in touch.
